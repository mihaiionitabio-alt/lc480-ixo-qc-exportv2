# Bibliography: scope and verification

Updated 21 November 2026. The article subset contains 19 journal articles with documented direct use in calculations, method definitions, adapted examples, implemented metadata profiles, technical reporting or manuscript methodology. Evidence for each inclusion is in `direct-use-evidence.json`.

The former 318-entry broad harvest is superseded. General background articles and references found only in other works are excluded. The user subsequently requested that identified GitHub resources and agency documents also be retained; those are included as distinct record types, without claiming direct use. Non-agency manuals and books remain excluded. Existing software licences and third-party acknowledgements remain intact outside this article-only bibliography.

The title/DOI audit inspected 824 source contexts covering 249 distinct DOIs, checked the former catalogue's registered identities, and screened all 318 entries for direct use. Context matching was a review aid, not an automatic finding of error. Three former DOI entries lacked usable source-context evidence and were not retained on metadata alone. Non-DOI materials were removed from this article-only scope. Bibliographic identity checks cannot establish whether every scientific claim is correct.

One confirmed wrong-work citation was found: a manuscript linked the title of Untergasser et al.'s raw-data-disclosure article to 10.3390/ijms24119175. The unrelated article is removed. The intended article is Molecular Oncology 17(5), pp. 713-717, DOI 10.1002/1878-0261.13418. The retained record already carried this correct DOI; it is not duplicated. No additional wrong-work title/DOI mismatch was confirmed among the retained articles.

Every retained article has registry-sourced bibliographic metadata and an explicit use record. Newly recovered direct citations include adopted examples and assay papers that were missing from the broad registry harvest. Source titles such as short method labels are not mistaken for publication titles.

Use `ixo-bibliography.bib` for BibTeX/biblatex; `urldate` records access separately from publication year. The Harvard reading list uses author initials and article titles. Access dates refer to DOI-registry metadata retrieval; individual publisher landing pages may block automated access. No date is inferred from local file modification times.

Private SOPs, laboratory identifiers, private paths and chat transcripts are excluded. The requested private Claude chats remain inaccessible and are not claimed as reviewed. The catalogue does not assert that listing a cited method means its entire implementation was copied. Claims about reproduction or biological validity require separate verification.

## Additional requested resources

The final list contains 99 records: 19 directly used articles, 31 identified GitHub repositories and 49 identified agency/CCSDS documents. Repository entries are deduplicated by owner/name from the project credits and recovered reference catalogue; individual source-file URLs are not counted as separate repositories. This does not include every GitHub link anywhere on the internet or inaccessible chat content.

Agency documents were identified from supplied covers, NASA NTRS catalogue records and official framework documentation. Corporate authors, report numbers, issue years and draft/revision status are preserved. NASA publication years use catalogue publication dates, not accession-number years or ingest dates. Some conference documents have no established public URL and are cited from the supplied copy without inventing an access date. Failed URL retrieval is recorded in link-checks.json; a local cover check is not a claim of current online availability. Agency-authored documents and presentations are included even when they are not standards. The SwissCube academic thermal-model paper is not represented as an agency document.

Agency titles are reproduced accurately and do not characterize MISSY as a flight system or assert endorsement, certification or compliance. Original software licence and attribution notices remain outside this reference list and are not removed.
