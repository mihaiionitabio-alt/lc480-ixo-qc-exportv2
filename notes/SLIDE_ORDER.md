## Notes for the speaker

- Slides 5 and 6 are the vocabulary. If the audience already knows control charts, drop both and go from 4 to 7.
- Slides 7-15 are lists. Show them, name two or three lines, move on — they exist so the deck can be handed out afterwards.
- Slide 28 must be said out loud before any range is shown: the limits are measured from six laboratory experiments and eighteen vendor demonstration files, and are invented inside the stated theoretical limit only where neither corpus reports the quantity.
- Slide 35 is the honesty slide: the demonstration history is synthetic.
- The shortest usable cut is 1, 2, 4, 16, 27, 36 (six slides).
